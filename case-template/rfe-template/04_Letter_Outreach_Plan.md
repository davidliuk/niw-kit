## Mandatory Rules for Every Letter

1. Paragraph 1 states the writer's position and the relationship (or that
   there is none) and how they encountered the work.
2. Concrete use only, in the writer's own words, with the exact type: ran
   released code; built on output or artifacts; reimplemented; compared
   against; classified in a survey. No upgrading.
3. No statutory boilerplate ("exceptional ability", "national interest
   waiver") and no general praise of the field.
4. Signed and dated; letterhead where the writer's employer allows it,
   otherwise a personal-capacity statement with title and institution; CV or
   bio page attached.
5. Collaborator letters state only what is true about cadence, joint work,
   and resources; never imply employment, funding, or sponsorship that does
   not exist, and never tell a writer that the officer criticized an earlier
   letter unless the notice says so.

## Not Suitable

- Anyone at the applicant's current employer when the brief argues impact
  beyond the employer (unless the letter's only function is role facts).
- Advisors or supervisors offered as "independent".
- Paid opinion services.
