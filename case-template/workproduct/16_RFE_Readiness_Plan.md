## 4. On Receipt of a Notice

1. Save the notice in `source_documents/`; record the printed due date in
   `status` tracking and the RFE folder README.
2. Copy `case-template/rfe-template/` to `cases/{{CASE_ID}}/rfe-{{NOTICE_DATE}}/`.
3. Table every finding and listed factor (`02_Response_Map.md`) before drafting.
4. Mark which elements the notice accepts; do not reargue them.
5. Pull the matching rows from Section 1; start outreach from Section 3 within
   one week.
6. Refresh `03_Policy_Sources.md` before writing any legal text.

## 5. Stop Rules

- Do not manufacture letters, users, metrics, awards, or job duties.
- Do not describe a post-filing fact as existing on the filing date.
- Do not alter screenshots or source documents except labeled redactions.
- Do not disclose confidential employer information without authorization.
- Do not change the locked endeavor in any response.
