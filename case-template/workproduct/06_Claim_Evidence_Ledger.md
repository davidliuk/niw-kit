## Evidence Classes

| Class | Examples | Can prove | Cannot prove alone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Objective primary record | Official transcript, published paper, commit history, HR letter, government report | The fact the record states on its face | Individual role in team work; significance |
| Third-party record of use | Citing paper describing how it used the work; downstream repository; integration notice | That a named party used the work in a stated way | Breadth beyond that party |
| First-hand letter | Collaborator, user, or expert letter | What the writer personally did or observed | Facts outside the writer's knowledge; legal conclusions |
| Applicant declaration | H-1, H-2 | Intent, plan, and role (corroborated elsewhere) | Anything an objective record could show |
| Context | Policy reports, market studies, field surveys | The importance of the problem or field | The importance of this endeavor |
