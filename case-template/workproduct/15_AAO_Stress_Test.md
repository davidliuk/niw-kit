## 1. Method

1. **Read the controlling law first.** Matter of Dhanasar; USCIS Policy
   Manual, Vol. 6, Part F, Ch. 5 (note the "current as of" date on the page);
   8 CFR 204.5(k).
2. **Find recent decisions yourself.** Use the USCIS AAO non-precedent
   decisions search (uscis.gov, Administrative Appeals Office decisions),
   filter to the EB-2 category for advanced-degree professionals and
   exceptional ability, and narrow by year. Search terms that surface close
   cases: your field, "national interest waiver", "software engineer",
   "research scientist", "open source", "proposed endeavor".
3. **Pick close comparators, sustained and dismissed.** Same field, same
   career stage, same kind of evidence. Read at least
   {{MIN_DECISIONS_TO_READ}} decisions from the last two years.
4. **Ask which evidentiary link was present or missing**, not who had more
   papers or citations:

   > specific endeavor → method and outputs → applicant's demonstrated
   > capacity → prospective impact beyond one employer → evidence for each link

5. **Fill the pattern table (Section 3).** For each pattern: how it would
   apply to this record, the mitigation already in the record (exhibit +
   page), and the residual risk.
6. **Do not compute approval rates** from the decisions list. Appeals are a
   biased sample, publication lags, and your selection is not random.
7. **Re-run** after any material change to the record and once on the
   frozen filing build.
