## 2. Form I-140

Official page: https://www.uscis.gov/i-140 (form, instructions, edition, filing tips). Verified on {{DATE}}; edition seen: {{EDITION}}.

- [ ] Download the edition USCIS lists on the filing date. Check on the form page which software opens the PDF; USCIS fillable forms may open only in Adobe Acrobat / Reader.
- [ ] Part 2: select the NIW item (advanced degree or exceptional ability). Item numbering: check on filing day.
- [ ] Part 1 self-petitioner questions: check the USCIS I-140 page and form instructions for how a self-petitioner answers the nonprofit-research and employee-count questions that set the Asylum Program Fee.
- [ ] Parts 1 and 3: legal name, addresses, identifiers match the source documents and the online-account profile.
- [ ] Part 4: choose adjustment or consular processing, not both; foreign address and native-script name if requested.
- [ ] Part 5: current occupation (may differ from Part 6). Annual income from the private record; the income figure goes on the form, not into the exhibits.
- [ ] Part 6: job title, SOC code, nontechnical description consistent with the endeavor sentence (see `10_Online_I140_Filing_Control.md`).
- [ ] Part 7: every spouse and child, even if not immigrating.
- [ ] Part 11: continuation text for every box that overflows and every Part 5 / Part 6 difference.
- [ ] Print all pages; sign and date the signature part in **handwritten ink**; scan the complete form into one PDF; keep the original.

Sources:

- [ ] Form ETA-9089 **Appendix A** completed (foreign-worker education and experience). Preserve every page.
- [ ] Form ETA-9089 **Final Determination**, uncertified, signed where required. It is not sent to DOL.
- [ ] Read on filing day whether USCIS also wants the main Form ETA-9089 itself. Follow the current form instructions and checklist; if official pages conflict, record the conflict and resolve it before filing. Record your decision: {{DECISION}}.

- [ ] Appendix A content matches Form I-140, the CV, and Exhibits B and C. Leave optional sections blank rather than padding them.

- [ ] Confirm NIW I-140 is eligible and read the current adjudication time frame on the I-907 page. Premium processing guarantees an action (approval, RFE, NOID, denial), not an approval; an RFE stops the clock.

## 8. Translations (8 CFR 103.2(b)(3))

For each foreign-language document:

- [ ] Clear copy of the complete original.
- [ ] Full English translation, including seals, stamps, handwritten notes, certificate numbers, and labels.
- [ ] Translator certification: competent to translate; translation complete and accurate.
- [ ] Translator printed name, signature, date, contact information.
- [ ] Consistent rendering of institution and award names across all exhibits.
- [ ] Translator is not the applicant (avoid self-certification).
- [ ] Freeze (hash) the preparation copy you give the translator; record the signed packet's hash. If either changes, the translator re-signs.
- [ ] Original + translation + certification in one PDF under the same exhibit ID.
